## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **PhD student in Computer Science at [Wayne State University](https://engineering.wayne.edu/)**, advised by **[Prof. Dongxiao Zhu](https://dongxiaozhu.github.io/)**.

My research lies at the intersection of **multimodal learning** and **trustworthy AI**, with much of my current work centered on **Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs)**.

I work on building multimodal systems that can **reason reliably, preserve privacy, and remain robust**.

My current research spans:

* 🔐 **Privacy-Aware Vision-Language Reasoning**
* 🛡️ **Adversarial Robustness & Security**
* 🎥 **Egocentric Video Understanding & VideoQA**
* 🧠 **Multimodal Memory & Retrieval**
* 🤖 **Large Vision-Language Models**

> **Research goal:** Building trustworthy multimodal systems that can reason reliably, preserve privacy, and remain robust.

## 🔬 Current Research

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔐 Privacy-Aware Multimodal AI

Studying how vision-language models can distinguish between **useful reasoning** and **privacy leakage**, with an emphasis on controllable privacy boundaries and trustworthy model behavior.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🛡️ Adversarial Robustness

Investigating **adversarial attacks and defenses** for deep learning and multimodal systems, with a focus on improving model reliability under malicious or unexpected inputs.

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎥 Egocentric Video Intelligence

Developing methods for **long-horizon egocentric video understanding**, VideoQA, memory construction, and retrieval over extended first-person visual experiences.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧠 Trustworthy LVLMs

Exploring reasoning, privacy, robustness, memory, and reliability in **Large Vision-Language Models** and other multimodal foundation models.

</td>
